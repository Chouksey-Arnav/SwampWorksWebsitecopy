How it works

From stuck to sorted in three steps

  1. 1

    Find a tutor

    Search by subject or browse everyone. Each profile shows what they teach and when they're free.

  2. 2

    Pick a time

    Tap an open time and confirm. It's booked instantly, and you can cancel up to a couple of hours before.

  3. 3

    Chat and meet

    Message your tutor about what you need. Officers can read chats, so everyone stays safe.
